- Changed the meaning of "min" and "max" for all angles in all locations (labels, metadata tables, global index files)
  to handle wraparound. For example, if the range of longitudes is really 350 to 10, then instead of 0-360, which we
  used to do, it now says min=350 and max=10. Thus it is possible for min > max.
